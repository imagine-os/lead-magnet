# C-03 · Departments board

## Purpose

Their departments, taken from the industry catalog rather than a generic template, as a board: each column has a KPI, the people who work there and the items open right now.

## Reference items (changelog `0025-os-demo-depth.md`, T62)

- **One KPI per department.** The board reads `industryFor(prospect)`, so the columns are the sub-industry's: eight for the dog hotel (Front desk · Hotel · Grooming & Spa · Daycare · People & pets · Payments & invoices · Messages & reviews · Marketing), nine for the tenant-law firm, eight for the wellness club. Each column shows the KPI at its own index where the sub names enough of them (`kpis[i]`), and only wraps past the end - no more `kpis[i % len]` repeating the same three numbers.
- **Open items rotate.** The three items per column are taken from the resolved pains plus two house items, rotated by column, so nine departments do not all show the same three lines.
- **Responsive.** `.demo-board` is `auto-fit, minmax(276px, 1fr)` and needed no change for nine columns: measured 1 column at 360 / 390, 2 at 768, 3 at 1280, 4 at 1920-3840, nothing past the viewport in light or dark.
