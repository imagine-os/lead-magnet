# C-03 · Departments board

## Purpose

Their departments, taken from the industry catalog rather than a generic template, as a board: each column has a KPI, the people who work there and the items open right now.

4. open items from the industry's pains

## Logic

- Departments, pains and KPIs come from `industry(prospect.industry)`.
- People are seeded with a stable hash so the same department always shows the same faces across reloads and languages.

## Components

`Card`, `Stat`, `Badge`, `Avatar`, `Button`, `Placeholder`.

## Real vs mock

Real: the department list and KPIs for the industry. Mocked: the people and the open items. Not wired: the department workspace behind "Open department".

## Responsive check (P-01)

Checked at: 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840. Sidebar below 900 px becomes a six-item bottom nav (labels ellipsise at 360); the widgets, board and inbox grids collapse to one column; `DataTable` stacks into cards under 768; at 1920 and above `--scale` grows type and spacing and the demo widens the sidebar, the frame and the widget columns for 10-foot reading.
