## What changed

### Cross-module contracts
- **Slots** (`src/engine/slots.ts`, pure, exported from `src/engine`): `tzForProspect`, `slotGrid` (deterministic FNV-1a availability, 09:00-17:00 local, lunch blocked, weekends closed), `previewSlots(grid, perDay = 3)` (first free of the morning, first after lunch, last of the afternoon), `withRequested(grid, iso)` (any valid instant is kept; outside the grid or generated busy it is appended / freed and flagged `requested`), `normalizeSlotIso`, `findSlot`, `slotInWords`, `dayLabel`, `timeLabel`, `zonedInstant`. Two engine checks guard it (`npm run test:engine`: 11 checks).

- **Language**: `I18nProvider` keeps the stored choice (`leadmagnet.lang`, only written by the toggle) separate from a page default; `useDefaultLang(prospect.lang)` on the landing pages and the demo shell makes Daniel's Spanish-first page and OS open in Spanish (`html lang="es"`) until the viewer presses EN / ES.
