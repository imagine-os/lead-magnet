# W-01 · Website home

## Purpose

Imagine's own public home for this offer, in our brand (default tokens, never a prospect's palette) with our own chrome. It makes one claim - an operating system already built for you - and proves it with a seeded prospect's system on phone, laptop and the back-office screen, the average saving across the systems on the page, and a grid of samples that all open live.

## Rules

- `R-W01` - the teaser prices come from `PRICE_MONTHLY`, never typed
- `R-W02` - every sample links to a real seeded page (`pages.slug`), so no example can go dead

## Logic

- Samples = seeded prospects joined to their `pages` row; the average saving is the mean of `savings(prospect, guesses).net_annual` across them, so the proof moves with the seed instead of being copy.
- The hero phone is the real landing page in a `PhoneFrame` (scaled iframe, same origin); the laptop and TV show `SamplePreview`, a drawn preview of that prospect's OS themed with `prospectStyle`, sized by container queries so it stays legible from 360 px to a 4K TV.
- Action handlers read the current sample through a ref, so `site.seeSample` works from the voice / WebMCP surface even before the visitor touches the switcher.

## Components

## Real vs mock

Real: the samples, their pages and demos, the savings maths, the prices, tracking. Nothing on this page is a placeholder; the honest list of what is not wired lives on W-02 in dev mode, and checkout is the Placeholder on W-03.

## Responsive check (P-01)

Checked at 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840. Hero becomes one column under 1100 px; devices stack under 900 px; grids collapse through the global breakpoints; the drawn previews scale with the device frame; nav wraps rather than hides (no hamburger, nothing hover-only).
