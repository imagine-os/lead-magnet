# B-02 · Booking confirmed

## Purpose

Says the slot back to the prospect in words they can check, tells them what happens next, and sends them into their demo rather than to a dead end. Same slim chrome and palette as B-01.

## Rules

- `R-B02` - the duration shown is the row's `duration_min`
- `R-B03` - the row is read back by id, never passed through navigation state

## Logic

- Slot in words: `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in the prospect's zone and the current language ("Monday, September 21 at 9:30 AM CT" / "lunes, 21 de septiembre a las 9:30 CT").
- With no booking on file the page offers B-01 instead of pretending.

## Real vs mock

Real: the row, the wording, the links. Not wired: the calendar file and provider link (T42). Nothing is faked - the Placeholder says what it will do and who builds it.
