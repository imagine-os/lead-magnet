# C-02 · Role home

## Purpose

One role's view of the business: the widgets `deriveRoleViews` gives that role, rendered by kind and themed to the industry, with a today strip and quick actions. Same component as C-01 with the role read from the URL, so a role switch is a real navigation people can share, bookmark and go back from.

## Logic

- Role slugs are unique per view (`viewSlug`): a business role keeps the bare slug (`owner`), a life role that collides gets `life-` in front (`life-owner`), so every one of the prospect's views is reachable and the role `Select` has one option per view. The page opens in `prospects.lang` unless the viewer chose EN / ES (D-034).

- The `:role` param is a slug (`front-desk`, `spouse-partner`); an unknown slug falls back to the first business role instead of erroring.
- The calendar widget's sample times become a seeded week of events built from the industry's departments and KPIs, so two prospects in the same industry still read differently.
- The chart is inline SVG with a text alternative listing the values - no chart library and no hover-only information.

Real: role derivation, theming, tracking, routing. Mocked: widget sample content from the engine plus seeded week events. Not wired: opening a widget full-screen.

## Responsive check (P-01)

Checked at: 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840. Sidebar below 900 px becomes a six-item bottom nav (labels ellipsise at 360); the widgets, board and inbox grids collapse to one column; `DataTable` stacks into cards under 768; at 1920 and above `--scale` grows type and spacing and the demo widens the sidebar, the frame and the widget columns for 10-foot reading.
