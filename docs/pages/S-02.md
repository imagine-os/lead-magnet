# S-02 · Prospect profile + AI intake

## Purpose

Everything we know about one prospect, and the conversational intake that closes the gaps. The panel asks **one question at a time** - the heaviest unknown first - with the reason it matters and the confidence points answering would add; every answer is saved with `applyAnswer()` and kept as a transcript turn, so the profile can always say who filled each field. The confidence meter and the recommended archetype recompute on every answer. The stack guesses are confirmed or rejected here (with likely / possible tiers), the roles and brand style that theme every prospect surface are edited here, and pasted website copy or call notes are turned into **proposals** a person confirms.

## Rules

- `R-S03` - every write is `update('prospects' | 'stack_guesses', id, patch)`
- `R-E03` - confidence is the weighted share of known fields; the intake asks the heaviest unknown first
- `R-E01` - the guesses being confirmed / rejected come from `guessStack()`; `savings()` ignores rejected rows
- `R-S05` - pasted facts are proposed with their evidence and never auto-applied
- `R-S06` - every answer appends an `intake_turns` row (field, question as asked, answer as saved, source, confidence after)

## Logic

- One question at a time: `nextQuestions(p, 12)` minus the ones skipped in this session; Skip never loses a question (there is a "bring them back" action when the queue empties).
- Control per question type (`src/modules/studio/fields.ts`): select for `industry / warmth / lang / revenue_band`, number for `team_size / locations`, comma list for `known_tools / business_roles / life_roles`, read-only summary plus "mark as known" for `style`, text otherwise. List and role questions offer one-tap suggestion chips from the industry catalog.
- Each question shows what it changes (`studio.effect_<field>`) and the confidence points it would add (`computeConfidence(fields_known + field) - confidence`).
- Paste facts: `extractFacts(text, prospect)` reads city (`City, ST` or "based in X"), team size ("team of 12", "12 employees", "somos 8"), locations, tool names matched against `CATALOG_TOOLS` longest-first, the language of the copy and the website URL. Each proposal carries the words that produced it; **Apply** writes it through the same `answer()` path with `source: 'rule'`, **Dismiss** drops it.
- "Ask the AI" runs `defaultEnricher` (rules) and records one transcript turn per filled field with `source: 'rule'`. "Research it for me" is the LLM path and stays a `Placeholder` (T43).
- Saving: `applyAnswer(p, field, value)` (pure) then one `update` with the field, `fields_known` and `confidence`.
- `pickArchetype(p, { guessedTools })` re-ranks on every change; its top row is the recommendation.
- The mini preview sets `prospectStyle(palette, font)` on its root and only reads `--lp-*`; the platform tokens never fork (P-12).
- "Enrich with AI" in the enrichment card is a `Placeholder` (T43). "Fill obvious gaps" runs `defaultEnricher` (the rule enricher) and marks the filled fields known.
- Stack tiers: `stackTier(guess)` is `likely` at or above 0.5 confidence (and always for a confirmed tool), `possible` below it (D-056 proposed).
