# R-01 · Client proposal view

## Purpose

The whole offer for one prospect on one page: what they get (their OS surfaces on phone, laptop and the back-office screen), what they save (their guessed stack against our price band), who gets a view (every business and life role), how it runs (phases bound by dependencies), the price, and the next step. Themed to the prospect with `--lp-*` and printable as a document, so a strategist can hand it over on a walkthrough call or print it for a board that still reads paper.

## Sections (layout order)

1. Toolbar (not printed) - Imagine wordmark, EN/ES, Print
2. Cover - business, prepared for (first + last name), prepared by, date, the band they fall into, a brand rule in their palette
3. What you get - phone / laptop / TV composition of their OS plus the seven surfaces (public website, customer app, staff and admin dashboards, documentation, operations manual, developer tools, testing hub), one line each, personalised with the business name
4. What it replaces - four stats (software today, tools cut, your plan, net saving) and the stack table with each cost struck through and what replaces it
5. Who gets a view - one card per business and life role from `deriveRoleViews`, with the widgets that role opens on
6. How it runs - six phases (intake, compose, demo, walkthrough, build passes, go-live), each naming the phase it depends on; "bound by dependencies, not calendar days"
7. Price - band, why that band (team size and locations), what is included, the comparison against today's spend; dev mode adds the D-014 proposed note
8. Next steps - the three steps in words, then Book the walkthrough (B-01), Accept proposal (Placeholder), Print, Open the live demo (C-01)

## Rules

- `R-R01` - every number on the page comes from `savings()` / `guessStack()`; nothing is typed
- `R-R02` - the page is a printable document (print stylesheet hides chrome, breaks between sections, black on white)
- `R-C06` - view, section_view and cta_click are tracked with `prospect_id` and the live `page_id`

## Logic

- Guesses: stored `stack_guesses` rows win; when a prospect has none (a fresh prospect in the studio) the engine guesses on the fly, so the proposal is never empty.
- `savings()` supplies monthly_current, annual_current, tools_cut, our_price_monthly, price_band, net_monthly, net_annual. Rejected guesses are excluded by the engine, not by the page.
- Band copy is chosen by `sv.price_band`; the "why" line quotes the prospect's team size and locations.
- Roles: `deriveRoleViews(prospect)` in engine order (business roles, then life roles); each card lists the widgets that role opens on, bilingual through `bi()`.
- Print: `proposal.print` fires `cta_click` then `window.print()`, so printing is measurable and is an action in the manifest (voice: "print the proposal").

## Components

LangToggle, Card, Stat, Badge, Button, Placeholder, DeviceMockup, DataTable, EmptyState. The device screens are content compositions inside `DeviceMockup` (no iframes, so the page prints).
